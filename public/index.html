Little Screen shows your children videos from the channels you allow, and nothing else. No recommendations rabbit hole, no Shorts unless you want them, and a daily time limit you set from your own phone.

How it works

Set it up once, change it any time

1

You choose the channels

Sign in to the parent dashboard on your own phone or computer and add the channels your child may watch.

2

Install it on your child's device

Use the Android app, or install Little Screen from the browser on iPhone, iPad and computers. Link it with a 6-digit code.

3

They watch, you stay in charge

Your child sees only those channels. If they find something else, they tap “Ask a grown-up” and you approve or say no.

Guide for parents

How to use Little Screen

1. Create your parent account

Go to the parent dashboard and sign up with email or Google. Use it from your own phone or computer, not your child's.

2. Choose channels

In Allowed channels, paste a channel's @handle, its link, or any video link from it. Tap a suggestion to start quickly. Use Blocked channels for channels that must never appear.

3. Set the rules

  • Daily watch time: minutes per day for each device.
  • Block Shorts and only made-for-kids videos.
  • Blocked words: hide videos whose titles contain them.
  • Device PIN: lets you add time on the child's device.

4. Link your child's device

Install Little Screen on their device (see above). In Devices, tap Get a pairing code and type it on their device. The code works once, for 15 minutes.

5. Answer requests

When your child asks for a video, it shows under Requests. Approve just that video, allow the whole channel, block it, or dismiss. Link Telegram in Settings to answer with one tap from your phone.

6. Lock the device

Little Screen works best when it's the only way to watch. Remove the YouTube app and block other browsers with Screen Time (iPhone, iPad) or Family Link (Android). If your network filters sites, allow:

  • youtube-nocookie.com
  • youtube.com
  • googlevideo.com
  • ytimg.com
  • googleapis.com
  • this site
Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Little Screen is free and open source. There are no ads and no paid features.

Can my child still reach the rest of YouTube?

Inside Little Screen they only see what you allowed. YouTube's own player still has a YouTube logo link, so also use your device's parental controls (step 6 above) to block the YouTube app and other browsers.

What data do you keep?

Your email, your rules, device names and today's total watch minutes. Nothing is collected from children and there's no tracking. Read the privacy policy. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Why is the Android app a download and not on Google Play?

The APK lets you start straight away. It's built automatically from the public source code, so anyone can check what's inside.

Why no App Store app for iPhone?

On iPhone and iPad, installing from Safari gives the same app with its own home-screen icon, without waiting for App Store review.

Can my organization run its own copy?

Yes. The code is open source and runs on a small server (for example Railway) with your own YouTube API key. See the source code for the setup guide.

Is this made by YouTube?

No. Little Screen is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Videos play in YouTube's official player under the YouTube Terms of Service.
