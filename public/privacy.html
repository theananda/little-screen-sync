Little Screen lets parents choose which YouTube channels and videos their children can watch. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it. We built Little Screen to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Parent account: your email address and a securely hashed password. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Your rules: the channels you allow or block, videos you approve, blocked words, daily time limits, your time zone, and a hashed device PIN.
  • Devices: the name you give each paired device (for example "Mia's iPad"), when it last connected, and the total minutes watched today.
  • Requests: when a child asks for a video, we store which video was requested, from which device, and your answer. Request history older than what you see in the dashboard may be removed.
  • Telegram (optional): if you link Telegram, we store your Telegram chat ID so we can send you requests.

What we don't collect

  • We do not ask children for any personal information. Children never create accounts, type their name, or sign in.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising identifiers, or sell or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics or third-party tracking in the child app.
  • We do not record which individual videos a child watched; only total minutes for the daily limit.

YouTube

Little Screen uses YouTube API Services to look up channels and videos, and plays videos in YouTube's own player (using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com). By using Little Screen you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we receive from YouTube (titles, channel names, thumbnails, durations) is stored for at most 30 days and then refreshed or deleted.

How we use data

Only to run Little Screen: to show your children the videos you allowed, sync your rules to their devices, enforce time limits, and deliver requests to you. We don't use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Data is stored on servers run by our hosting provider, Railway, in [region]. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the parent dashboard under Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, rules, devices and request history right away. Removing a device in the dashboard deletes that device's record.

Children's privacy

Little Screen is designed for families and follows the principles of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws: we collect no personal information from children, and parents control all settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents by email.
