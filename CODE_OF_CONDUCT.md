# Code of Conduct

Little Screen is built by and for families from many countries, languages and backgrounds. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and safe.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or personal attacks, in issues, discussions, pull requests or anywhere you represent the project.
- Never share anyone's private information, including details about children.

## Reporting

If someone behaves badly, email **[conduct contact email]**. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban people who break these rules, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
